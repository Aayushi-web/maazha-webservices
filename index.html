Our process

A clear path from your idea to real impact

You stay involved at every milestone, from the first conversation through launch and what comes next.

PHASE 01

Understand your business

1

In a kickoff, we map your customers, tools, workflows, and pain points, then agree on the problem and how success will be measured.

PHASE 02

Agree on the right plan

2

We recommend the right AI, automation, app, or marketing solution, then agree on scope, milestones, timeline, and responsibilities before work begins.

PHASE 03

Create it together

3

We design and build in milestones, share working previews, and use your feedback to shape features, integrations, and workflows.

PHASE 04

Test, refine & approve

4

Your team tests real scenarios across devices and integrations. We resolve issues, refine the details, and get your approval before launch.

PHASE 05

Launch & keep improving

5

We deploy, document, and guide your team through handoff. Together, we track results, provide support, and plan improvements.
